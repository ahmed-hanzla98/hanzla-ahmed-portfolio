Case Study

Programmatic Yield & Reporting

Consolidated Ad Ops Performance Dashboard

Scattered reporting across multiple DSPs and SSPs makes it hard to see where fill rate or eCPM is slipping. This dashboard pulls partner-level bid data into one view — revenue and eCPM trend, fill rate vs. win rate by partner, and a floor price vs. bid density chart used to guide yield tuning decisions.

Approach
Unified DSP/SSP reporting into partner-level KPIs: fill rate, win rate, eCPM, revenue.
Focus
Floor price vs. bid density analysis to guide yield and floor-pricing decisions.
Output
A single dashboard stakeholders can read in seconds instead of cross-referencing spreadsheets.

Sample dashboard built for portfolio purposes — figures are illustrative, not client data.
